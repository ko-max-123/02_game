YTC STANDALONE COMBAT PROTOTYPE

HOW TO START
1. Copy this entire YTC_StandalonePrototype folder to a Windows PC.
2. Double-click YTC_CombatDemo.exe.
3. Unity Editor and Unity Hub are not required.

CONTROLS
- A / D: Move left / right
- W / S: Move within the limited depth lane
- Space tap: Jump
- Space hold: Jet flight
- Left Mouse Button: Aim and fire
- J: Fire in the facing direction
- R: Restart the battle
- Esc: Quit

DISTRIBUTION NOTE
Keep YTC_CombatDemo.exe, YTC_CombatDemo_Data, UnityPlayer.dll,
UnityCrashHandler64.exe, MonoBleedingEdge, D3D12, and the other files together.
Do not distribute the EXE by itself.
